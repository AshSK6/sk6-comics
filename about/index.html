WHO THE FUCK IS SK6?

We make comics
because we want to.

SK6 is an independent comics and art project built by two artists who wanted to make their own stories, their own way.

No publisher telling us what to make. No algorithm deciding what a comic should be. Just ideas, drawings, stories, characters, and a lot of time spent making things we actually want to exist.
